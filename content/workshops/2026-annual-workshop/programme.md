> Registration is now open! [Please register here!](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/ppig-2026-tickets-1992339085838)

The PPIG Doctoral Consortium will take place on Monday morning. This is an invitation-only event for accepted doctoral students and a small panel of faculty mentors.

The main PPIG conference this year will then start at lunchtime on 27th July until late afternoon of Wednesday 29th of July. All sessions will run in a hybrid format, both via zoom and in-person.

Below you can find our working outline of the programme so you can roughly plan your attendance. The detailed programme which may include some minor changes to scheduling will be announced next month.

> Note: All times are in BST (UTC+1, UK's timezone)
